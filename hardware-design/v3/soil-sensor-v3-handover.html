Design scope

FunctionBattery-capable soil sensor node with an 868 MHz LoRa link.
ControllerESP32-S3-WROOM-1 module with its integrated 2.4 GHz antenna.
SensorExternal Adafruit STEMMA soil sensor connected by 3.3 V I2C.
PowerUSB-C or two AA cells in series through an external holder. No charger.
Power cyclePCB-mounted 15-minute timer switches the ESP32, LoRa module and sensor off together.
ConnectorsUSB-C, battery and I2C connectors are fitted. Unused connectors remain empty.

System description

U6 reads the external soil sensor over I2C and communicates with U5 over SPI. U5 drives the 868 MHz antenna through a 50 Ω feed and matching network. The ESP32 module provides the 2.4 GHz antenna. J1 provides USB power and USB D+/D−; J3 connects to an external 2×AA holder.

U4 starts the switched 3.3 V rail every 15 minutes. A DONE edge from IO4 switches the rail off. U6, U5 and J2 are powered only from this rail and are unpowered between cycles. Only the signals listed as firmware-fixed on sheet 5 have mandatory GPIO numbers; the other GPIO numbers are recommendations.

PCB and manufacturing requirements

Layout

  • Use the minimum practical outline that satisfies component courtyards, both antenna keep-outs and the DN038 feed/ground geometry. Do not reduce the DN038 adjacent ground area.
  • Four layers, 0.8 mm finished thickness. Obtain the fabricator's released impedance stack-up before routing. Signals are on top and bottom; one inner layer is ground and the other is power. Remove both inner planes from the defined 868 MHz and 2.4 GHz antenna keep-out areas.
  • One antenna at each end, left and right, as far apart as the board allows.
  • USB-C on the bottom edge, with the battery and soil-sensor connectors on the top edge.
  • USB-C or two series-connected AA cells may power the board. USB takes priority and must not feed current into the cells. Do not fit a charging circuit.
  • The complete 3.3 V rail is switched. Use the timer and buck-boost wiring on sheet 3; do not leave the sensor or either radio on an unswitched supply.
  • ERC and DRC must report zero errors, including schematic parity. This is a condition of delivery.

Fabrication and assembly

  • Surface finish: lead-free HASL.
  • Ground vias: 0.3 mm finished hole, 0.6 mm pad. Stitch ground at 3 mm spacing or closer around both antenna keep-outs, along the 50 Ω feed and around the board edge. The DN038 helix inter-layer vias are separate antenna features: copy their 0.38 mm diameter from the TI Gerber and never connect them to GND.
  • Placement: modules, connectors, switches and the converter on top. Small resistors and capacitors may be placed on the bottom.

Timer requirement

Fit TPL5111DDCR directly on the PCB. Fit R13 as 68.4 kΩ, 1% for a nominal 900-second cycle. Switch the complete 3V3_SWITCHED rail so the ESP32, LoRa module and sensor lose power together.

Power and timer wiring all nets and parts are on the main PCB

Exact timer connections

U4 pinConnect to
1 · VDDBAT_AON; C13 0.1 µF to GND at the pin
2 · GNDGround
3 · DELAYR13 68.4 kΩ, 1% to GND; SW3 momentary to BAT_AON
4 · DONEQ2 collector; R12 100 kΩ pull-up to BAT_AON
5 · DRVnD5 anode A; D5 common cathode is PWR_EN
6 · ENHard-wire to BAT_AON for repeating timer mode

Q2 is an MMBT3904 level shifter: pin 1 base from IO4 through R14 47 kΩ, pin 2 emitter to GND and pin 3 collector to U4 DONE; fit R15 1 MΩ base-to-GND. This prevents BAT_AON from reaching the 3.3 V GPIO and guarantees U4's 0.7 × VDD high threshold. D5 is a BAT54C dual common-cathode diode: pin 1 anode to U4 DRVn, pin 2 anode to USB VBUS and pin 3 common cathode to PWR_EN and R16 100 kΩ to GND. Thus U4 controls battery operation, while any USB connection holds the board on.

Power input and converter

  • J3: JST S2B-PH-SM4-TB, 2 pin, side entry. Pin 1 BAT_RAW, pin 2 GND. Fit on every board; leave empty when unused.
  • Battery: external two-AA series holder; alkaline, NiMH or 1.5 V lithium primary. J3 range is 2.0 V minimum while loaded to 3.6 V maximum. Do not use a rectangular 9 V battery. Fit no charger.
  • Q1: Vishay Si2367DS-T1-GE3 P-channel MOSFET for reverse-battery protection. Pin 3 drain to BAT_RAW, pin 2 source to BAT_AON, pin 1 gate to GND through R20 1 MΩ.
  • U7: TPS2116DRLR power multiplexer. Pin 1 GND; pins 2 and 7 VIN_RAW; pin 3 USB_VBUS; pin 4 PR1; pin 5 MODE tied to USB_VBUS; pin 6 BAT_AON; pin 8 to a status test pad only. Make the PR1 divider with R21 330 kΩ from USB_VBUS to PR1 and R22 100 kΩ from PR1 to GND. Fit 1 µF to GND at VIN1, VIN2 and VOUT. This priority-mode wiring selects USB when present and blocks reverse current into either source.
  • U2: TPS631000DRLR adjustable buck-boost converter. Pin 4 VIN to VIN_RAW; pin 1 VOUT to 3V3_SWITCHED; pin 5 EN to PWR_EN; pin 6 MODE to GND for power-save mode; pin 7 to GND. Connect a 1 µH inductor between pins 3 LX1 and 2 LX2. Set 3.3 V with R23 511 kΩ from VOUT to pin 8 FB and R24 91 kΩ from FB to GND. Fit 22 µF at VIN and 47 µF at VOUT, placed as shown in the TI reference layout and selected so their effective capacitance remains within the datasheet limits. Fit R17 10 kΩ from 3V3_SWITCHED to GND.
  • Power domains: Q1 produces BAT_AON. Connect BAT_AON only to U4 VDD/EN, U7 VIN2 and the stated always-on pull resistors. U7 VOUT is VIN_RAW and connects to U2 VIN. U2 VOUT is 3V3_SWITCHED. Every ESP32, Wio-SX1262-N and sensor supply pin connects only to 3V3_SWITCHED.

The 15 minutes is measured between timer wake edges. If the active period takes 8 seconds, the power-off time is approximately 14 minutes 52 seconds. If DONE is never asserted, U4 keeps the board on until the end of the interval, removes power for about 50 ms, then starts the next cycle. With USB attached, D5 holds PWR_EN high, so a DONE edge does not switch off the board.

U4 switches off only when its DONE pin changes from low to high. Provide labelled test pads at IO4/Q2 base and U4 DONE so this edge can be checked without firmware. Do not connect IO4 directly to the always-on DONE net.

Bring-up acceptance

  • Design and verify the power path for 550 mA peak at 3.3 V and approximately 1 A from a 2.0 V pack. Calculate input bulk capacitance from the measured load transient and AA cell, holder and contact resistance.
  • On battery, verify a nominal 15-minute interval between power-up edges and document measured tolerance.
  • Without USB, after DONE, verify 3V3_SWITCHED falls below 0.3 V within 5 s; confirm the ESP32, LoRa module and sensor are unpowered.
  • Measure total battery current while powered off. Target under 5 µA at 25 °C, including input leakage and U2 shutdown current; record the actual result.
  • Press SW3 while powered off and confirm a wake within 40 ms. With 5 V USB attached, confirm the board stays on and current from USB into J3 is at most 5 µA at 25 °C.
  • Use a current-limited bench source at J3. Run a full sensor read and +22 dBm LoRa transmit while J3 remains at 2.0 V, then at 3.6 V. Capture droop at U2 VIN, U5 pad 8 and U6 pad 2; no brownout is allowed.
  • Repeat the low-voltage transmit test with representative partly discharged AA cells, including holder and contact resistance. Record the practical battery cutoff.

Required support wiring verify against the released manufacturer documents

  • U5: pads 8 to 3V3_SWITCHED, 7 and 10 to GND, and 9 to the 50 Ω RF path. Fit 100 nF and 10 µF beside pad 8. Provide series-damping footprints in SCK, MOSI, MISO and NSS; fit 22 Ω initially and verify the values during bring-up.
  • U6: pad 2 to 3V3_SWITCHED; pads 1, 40 and exposed pad 41 to GND. Use Espressif's reference decoupling. Pull EN up with 10 kΩ and fit 1 µF from EN to GND. Verify against the N8R8 module guidance.
  • J1: use the exact HRO mating-face names. Join A6+B6 as D+ and A7+B7 as D−. Join A4+A9+B4+B9 as USB_VBUS; join A1+A12+B1+B12 to GND. Fit separate 5.1 kΩ resistors from A5/CC1 and B5/CC2 to GND. Leave A8/B8 SBU unconnected. Connect all four shell tabs directly to board GND at the connector with short copper and multiple vias.
  • USB data: route D−/D+ as a 90 Ω differential pair through U1, then through series footprints at U6 to IO19/IO20. Fit 0 Ω initially; change to 22–33 Ω only after signal-integrity testing. Optional shunt capacitors are DNP. The design is a USB 2.0 sink with no PD controller; do not assume more than 500 mA from VBUS, and verify operation from a 5 V source current-limited to 500 mA.
  • U1 and U3: follow the USBLC6-2P6 flow-through pinout. Pin 2 is GND. Pin 5 connects to USB_VBUS for U1 and 3V3_SWITCHED for U3. Place each device beside its connector and fit 100 nF directly between pins 5 and 2.
  • User LED: connect the LED anode to 3V3_SWITCHED through 1 kΩ and its cathode to IO48. This makes the firmware-fixed LED active low.
  • Release checks: verify J3 polarity and mark it on copper and silkscreen. Keep the U2 switch node away from both antennas. Apply the antenna keep-outs on sheet 2 and tune the assembled 868 MHz antenna.
